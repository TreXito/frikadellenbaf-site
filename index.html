BAF handles the steps around a flip, from incoming data to in-game actions and remote visibility. These parts are documented in the public repository.

BEFORE YOU GO

Know the trade-offs.

Automating gameplay can violate Hypixel’s rules and lead to account penalties. Flip values are estimates, not confirmed profit until items sell. BAF is open source under AGPLv3 and is not affiliated with Hypixel, Mojang or Coflnet.
