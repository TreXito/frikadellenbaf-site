Frikadellen BAF is a headless Rust client for Auction House and Bazaar automation. It acts on Coflnet flip data, manages listings and orders, and gives you a real view of the bot through a web panel and Discord.

05 / THE FINE PRINT

A bot has
consequences.

Automating gameplay can violate Hypixel’s rules and lead to account penalties. Market conditions, latency and configuration affect outcomes; displayed flip values are not confirmed profit until items sell.

Frikadellen BAF is open source under AGPLv3 and is not affiliated with Hypixel, Mojang or Coflnet. The repository is the source of truth for current behavior.
